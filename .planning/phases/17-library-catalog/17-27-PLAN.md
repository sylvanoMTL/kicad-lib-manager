<objective>
A real isolation switch for owner checks (D-35). The 17-17 check pointed `$env:APPDATA` at a scratch folder, but the app's own settings (Qt asks Windows for that folder) and its backups (in the local app-data folder) still went to the real places. The switch moves the app's settings, saved scans, backups and KiCad's settings folder under one scratch folder; plans 17-19 and 17-20 are written to use it (17-20 only where it has a scratch step: it has none, so it states that the switch stays off).

Purpose: D-35, and through it the safety of the owner-run Würth rehearsal (CAT-12, D-25) and the live list check (CAT-09, D-37).
