Close UAT gap 1 for the three dialogs and remove the parking mechanism: the Choose Library Folder, first-run and New Collection windows check folders (and create a collection) on the background runner from plan 15-13, cancel at close instead of parking a QThread, and the join-then-park helper is deleted.

The design and its justification are in 15-13-PLAN.md (daemon threads, results delivered on the GUI thread by a queue and a QTimer, no QThread to destroy).
Output: the three dialogs on `run_in_background`; the helper, list and budget constant removed from import_dialog.py; three more child-process modes; an AST guard.
</objective>
